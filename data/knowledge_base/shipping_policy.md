# Pearlz Shipping Policy

Version 1.8. Last updated: 1 March 2026.

## 1. Where we deliver

Pearlz delivers to addresses in Pakistan only. International shipping is not offered at present.

All orders are delivered by our courier partner, Kestrel Couriers.

## 2. Delivery timelines

Delivery times are counted in working days (Monday to Saturday, excluding public holidays) from the day your order is dispatched.

| Region tier | Cities and areas | Delivery time after dispatch |
|---|---|---|
| Metro | Karachi, Lahore, Islamabad, Rawalpindi | 1 to 2 working days |
| Secondary cities | Faisalabad, Multan, Peshawar, Hyderabad, Gujranwala, Sialkot, Quetta, Bahawalpur, Sargodha | 2 to 4 working days |
| Remote areas | Gilgit, Skardu, Hunza, Chitral, Gwadar, Turbat, Zhob | 5 to 7 working days |

Addresses not listed above are assigned to a tier at checkout from their postcode. The tier and the expected delivery window are shown before you pay.

## 3. Dispatch cut-off times

- Orders confirmed by **1:00 pm** Pakistan time, Monday to Friday, are dispatched the same day.
- Orders confirmed by **11:00 am** on Saturday are dispatched the same day.
- Orders confirmed after the cut-off, or on a Sunday or public holiday, are dispatched on the next working day.

An order is confirmed when payment is received or, for cash on delivery, when you confirm the order by phone or SMS.

## 4. Shipping charges

| Region tier | Orders below PKR 15,000 | Orders of PKR 15,000 or more |
|---|---|---|
| Metro | PKR 350 | Free |
| Secondary cities | PKR 550 | Free |
| Remote areas | PKR 1,200 | PKR 1,200 |

The free-shipping threshold does not apply to remote areas.

## 5. Next-day delivery

Next-day delivery is offered in Karachi, Lahore and Islamabad for orders confirmed by 1:00 pm, Monday to Friday. It costs PKR 900, in addition to any standard shipping charge. Next-day delivery is not offered for orders placed on Saturday, Sunday or a public holiday.

## 6. Packaging and fragile items

Purifiers are shipped upright in double-wall cartons with moulded pulp inserts, marked "Fragile" and "This side up". Please check the carton when it arrives. If it is visibly damaged, you can refuse the delivery, or accept it and note the damage on the courier's delivery slip. Report any damage to the product within 48 hours of delivery, as described in the Refund & Returns Policy.

## 7. Failed delivery attempts

- The courier calls you before each delivery attempt.
- If nobody can receive the parcel, the courier tries once more on the next working day.
- After two failed attempts, the parcel is returned to the Pearlz warehouse. Redelivery costs PKR 400, unless the failure was the courier's fault.
- Cash-on-delivery orders are cancelled after two failed attempts.
- For prepaid orders that are returned and not redelivered within 14 days, Pearlz refunds the price paid minus the original shipping charge.

## 8. Tracking your order

When your order is dispatched, you receive an SMS and an email with your Kestrel Couriers tracking number. You can track the parcel on the Pearlz website under Track my order, on the Kestrel Couriers website, or in the Orders section of the Pearlz Home app.

## 9. Changing your delivery address

You can change the delivery address free of charge until the order is dispatched, by contacting support. After dispatch, the address can only be changed through Kestrel Couriers and may add to the delivery time.
