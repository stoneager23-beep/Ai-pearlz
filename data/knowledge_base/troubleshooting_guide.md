# Pearlz Troubleshooting Guide

Version 1.6. Last updated: 10 February 2026. Covers the AquaPearl 500 Pro, the AquaPearl 300 and the AeroPearl Mini.

Use this guide to find the likely cause of a problem and what you can safely do about it. Before you check anything inside the filter compartment, unplug the purifier and close the feed-water valve. If your problem is in the list of symptoms you must not fix yourself (Section 4), stop and contact Pearlz support.

## 1. Water purifiers: symptoms, causes and actions

| Symptom | Probable cause | What to do |
|---|---|---|
| No power: the display and all lights stay dark | Socket switched off or faulty; power cord loose; purifier in standby | Check the socket with another appliance. Push the cord firmly into the purifier. Hold POWER for 3 seconds. If the purifier still does not start, contact support. |
| Low flow from the nozzle or tap | Storage tank empty; PF-1 or CP-1 pre-filter blocked; low mains pressure; inlet tube kinked | Wait 40 minutes for the tank to refill. Check the feed-water valve is fully open and the tube is straight. If the pre-filter is due, replace it. |
| Water leaking around the base | Loose tube connection; drip tray overflowing; leak inside the housing | Dry the area and check each push-fit connection is pushed fully home. Empty the drip tray. If water keeps appearing under the purifier, or you see water inside the housing, see Section 4. |
| Water tastes metallic or salty | RO membrane at the end of its life; TDS has risen | Check the TDS reading. If it stays above 100 ppm, replace the RO membrane (RO-3 on the AquaPearl 500 Pro, RO-2 on the AquaPearl 300). |
| Water tastes stale or flat | Water has stood in the tank for more than 3 days | Dispense and discard 2 litres. If you are often away, see "When you go away" in the user manual. |
| Water smells of chlorine | Carbon stage exhausted | Replace the CB-2 carbon block (AquaPearl 500 Pro) or the PC-3 post-carbon filter (AquaPearl 300). |
| The purifier runs continuously and never stops | Tank level sensor stuck; inlet pressure too low for the membrane; leak in the tank outlet | Switch off for 10 minutes and on again. Check inlet pressure. If it keeps running for more than 2 hours, contact support. If it shows E-05, see Section 3. |
| Triple audible alert with the FILTER indicator pulsing red | Alert E-03 on the AquaPearl 500 Pro: sediment pre-filter saturation | Replace the PF-1 pre-filter and hold RESET for 5 seconds. See Section 3. |
| FILTER indicator pulsing red with no audible alert | A cartridge has reached the end of its counted life | Tap RESET on the AquaPearl 500 Pro, or open the app, to see which cartridge. Replace it and reset its counter. |
| FILTER light amber | A cartridge is due within 15 days | Order the cartridge now; the purifier keeps working normally. |
| TDS reading high: above 100 ppm on the display | RO membrane worn out; new membrane not yet settled; feed water above 2,000 ppm | A new membrane can read high for its first two tanks. Otherwise replace the RO membrane. Check your feed water with a TDS meter. |
| Noisy pump: louder than a soft hum | Purifier not level; wall bracket loose; pump wear | Check the purifier sits level and the bracket screws are tight. A grinding or rattling noise: see Section 4. |
| The app will not pair with the purifier | Router broadcasting only 5 GHz; purifier not in pairing mode; phone too far away; location permission off | Connect your phone to a 2.4 GHz network. Hold WI-FI for 3 seconds until the light blinks blue. Stay within 5 metres. Allow location permission during pairing. |
| Tank does not fill, or fills very slowly | Feed-water valve closed; pre-filter blocked; inlet pressure below 1.0 bar | Open the valve fully. Replace the pre-filter if due. Check your water pressure. |
| Water looks cloudy or milky | Fine air bubbles | Let the glass stand for one minute. If it clears from the bottom up, it was air and is harmless. If it does not clear, stop drinking the water and contact support. |
| Drip tray fills quickly | Dispensing nozzle dripping because of scale | Descale the nozzle cap in a vinegar solution as described in the user manual. |
| Display shows dashes or random characters | Display not communicating with the control board (E-08) | Unplug for 1 minute and plug in again. If it happens again, contact support. |
| UV light off or flashing blue (AquaPearl 500 Pro) | UV-LED module fault | Do not open the purifier. Contact support. |
| Purified water feels warm | Purifier installed near a heat source or in direct sunlight | Move the purifier away from the stove, oven or sunny window. |
| Water drips from the drain tube when the purifier is idle | Drain saddle clamp loose; waste-water valve not closing | Tighten the drain saddle clamp. If dripping continues for more than an hour after the tank is full, contact support. |

## 2. AeroPearl Mini air purifier

| Symptom | Probable cause | What to do |
|---|---|---|
| Air-quality light stays red | HEPA H13 filter due; room larger than 25 square metres; windows open | Check filter life in the app; the HEPA filter lasts 12 months. Close windows. Use the AeroPearl Mini in rooms up to 25 square metres. |
| Musty or stale smell from the outlet | Carbon pre-filter exhausted | Replace the carbon pre-filter; it lasts 6 months. |
| Fan louder than usual | Mesh pre-screen blocked with dust | Remove the mesh pre-screen and wash it in water; dry it fully before refitting. Wash it every month. |
| Will not switch on | Power adaptor not connected; filter door open | Check the adaptor. The AeroPearl Mini does not start if the filter door is open. |

## 3. Audible Alert Codes

The AquaPearl 500 Pro reports problems with an audible alert pattern, a red ALERT indicator and a code on the display. Tap any button to silence the alert tone.

| Code | What you hear | What it means | What to do |
|---|---|---|---|
| E-01 | Single long tone | No water reaching the purifier | Open the feed-water valve; straighten the inlet tube. The purifier restarts by itself. |
| E-02 | Double short tone, repeated every minute | Leak sensor in the base is wet | Unplug, close the feed-water valve and dry the base. If the code comes back after drying, see Section 4. |
| E-03 | Triple short tone; FILTER indicator pulses red | Sediment pre-filter (PF-1) saturation | Replace PF-1, then hold RESET for 5 seconds. |
| E-04 | Quadruple short tone | RO membrane producing too little water | Check the TDS. Replace the RO-3 membrane if TDS stays above 100 ppm or the tank takes more than 90 minutes to fill. |
| E-05 | Continuous tone for 10 seconds | Pump overheated | Unplug for 30 minutes. If it happens again, see Section 4. |
| E-06 | Double long tone | Inlet water pressure below 1.0 bar | Check your mains pressure. A booster pump may be needed; book a professional installation. |
| E-07 | Rapid five-pulse tone | Tank level sensor fault | Switch off and on. If it returns, contact support. |
| E-08 | Short tone every 2 seconds for 1 minute | Display and control board not communicating | Unplug for 1 minute. If it returns, contact support. |

The AquaPearl 300 has no alert codes; it signals a due pre-filter with its single FILTER light.

## 4. Problems you must not fix yourself

Stop using the purifier, unplug it, close the feed-water valve and contact Pearlz support if you notice any of the following:

- water inside the housing, or water that keeps appearing under the purifier after you have dried it;
- a burning smell, smoke or sparks;
- alert E-02 returning after you have dried the base;
- alert E-05 appearing again after the purifier has been unplugged for 30 minutes;
- a grinding or rattling noise from the pump;
- a UV-LED fault on the AquaPearl 500 Pro;
- any problem that would require opening the purifier housing.

Do not open the housing or ask a non-authorised technician to repair the purifier. This voids the warranty (Warranty Policy, Section 3.2). Pearlz treats these cases as top-priority tickets.
