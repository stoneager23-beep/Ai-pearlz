# Frequently Asked Questions

Last updated: 5 October 2026.

## Ordering and payment

### Can I order over the phone instead of the website?

Yes. Call us free on 0800-73275 (Monday to Saturday, 9:00 to 18:00) or message us on WhatsApp at +92 300 0732759 (every day, 9:00 to 21:00), and an agent will place the order for you.

### How can I pay?

You can pay by debit or credit card, by bank transfer, from a mobile wallet, or in cash when the parcel arrives. Cash on delivery is available for orders up to PKR 50,000.

### I put the wrong address on my order. Can I fix it?

Yes, as long as the order has not left our warehouse yet. Contact support and we will change it for free. Once it has been dispatched, the change has to go through the courier and can delay delivery.

### Do you ship outside Pakistan?

Not at the moment. We only deliver to addresses in Pakistan.

## Delivery

### When will my purifier arrive?

It depends on where you live. Karachi, Lahore, Islamabad and Rawalpindi usually get it in 1 to 2 working days after dispatch, other big cities in 2 to 4 working days, and remote areas such as Gilgit, Skardu or Gwadar in 5 to 7 working days. If you order before 1 pm on a weekday, we send it out the same day.

### Is delivery free?

For orders of PKR 15,000 or more, yes, everywhere except remote areas. Remote areas always pay a flat PKR 1,200. Smaller orders cost PKR 350 in the metro cities and PKR 550 in other cities.

### Can I get it tomorrow?

Next-day delivery costs PKR 900 and is available in Karachi, Lahore and Islamabad if you order before 1 pm, Monday to Friday.

### The courier came when I wasn't home. What now?

The courier calls before coming and will try again the next working day. If the second attempt also fails, the parcel goes back to our warehouse. We can send it again for PKR 400. Cash-on-delivery orders are cancelled after two missed deliveries.

### The box looked crushed when it arrived.

You can refuse the parcel, or accept it and write the damage on the courier's slip. Either way, send us photos within 48 hours and we will replace anything that is damaged.

## Installation

### Can I install the purifier myself?

Yes. Most people finish in 60 to 90 minutes by following the AquaPearl Installation Guide step by step. If you'd rather not, book a professional installation.

### How much does it cost to have someone install it?

A Pearlz technician charges PKR 2,500 in Karachi, Lahore, Islamabad and Rawalpindi and PKR 3,500 in other cities. In remote areas we can't send a technician for installation, but we'll guide you through it on a free video call.

### My water pressure is really strong. Is that a problem?

If it's above 4.0 bar, yes: fit a Pearlz pressure-reducing valve (PKR 3,200) before using the purifier. If your pressure is very weak, below 1.0 bar, you will need a booster pump, which our technicians fit.

### Can I hide the purifier under the sink?

The AquaPearl 300 can go under the sink if the cabinet is at least 45 cm high, 30 cm wide and 45 cm deep. The AquaPearl 500 Pro can't, because you need to reach its screen and buttons; mount it on the wall or stand it on the counter.

## Filters

### How often do I need to change the filters?

On the AquaPearl 500 Pro: the sediment pre-filter every 6 months, the carbon block and the mineraliser every 12 months, and the RO membrane every 24 months, or sooner if you use a lot of water. On the AquaPearl 300: the composite pre-filter every 6 months, the post-carbon filter every 12 months and the RO membrane every 24 months.

### How will I know it's time for a new filter?

The FILTER light turns amber about 15 days before a filter is due and starts flashing red when it's overdue. If you use the Pearlz Home app, it sends you a reminder too.

### Can I buy cheaper filters from the market?

Please don't. Non-Pearlz cartridges may not seal properly, can leak, and any damage they cause isn't covered by the warranty.

### My water tastes different from bottled water. Is something wrong?

Probably not. Each brand of bottled water has its own mineral content. The AquaPearl 500 Pro adds back a little calcium and magnesium after purification, so its water usually reads between 40 and 90 ppm on the display.

### Does the purifier waste a lot of water?

For every litre of drinking water, about one litre of concentrated water goes down the drain. That is normal for reverse-osmosis purifiers.

## Warranty

### How long is my warranty?

The AquaPearl 500 Pro has 24 months, the AquaPearl 300 has 12 months, and the AeroPearl Mini has 12 months, with 24 months on its fan motor. Your warranty starts from the date on your invoice.

### I lost my invoice. Am I still covered?

Yes. Without the invoice, we count your warranty from the manufacturing date printed on the label on the back of the unit.

### I bought my purifier second-hand. Does the warranty still count?

Yes, for whatever time is left, as long as you have the original invoice. Email it to us with the serial number and we'll put the warranty in your name.

### Are the filters covered by the warranty?

No. Filters wear out with use, so they are treated as consumables, like the tablets used for cleaning the tank.

### My old AquaPearl 250 has stopped working. Can you still fix it?

Yes. The AquaPearl 250 came with a 36-month Extended Care warranty, and we keep spare parts for it until 30 June 2029. If a part is no longer available while you're under warranty, we replace the unit with an AquaPearl 300.

## The Pearlz Home app and your account

### The app won't connect to my purifier.

Make sure your phone is on a 2.4 GHz Wi-Fi network; the purifier can't see 5 GHz-only networks. Hold the WI-FI button for 3 seconds until its light blinks, stay close to the purifier, and allow location access when the app asks.

### Do I have to use the app?

No. The purifier works perfectly without it. The app just shows you filter life and water-quality history and reminds you when filters are due.

### How do I update my purifier's software?

You don't need to do anything. When the purifier is connected to the app, updates install themselves overnight. You can check the version in the app under Settings, then Device info.

### How do I delete my account?

In the app, go to Settings, then Privacy, then Delete my account. You can also email privacy@pearlzhome.example. We reply within 30 days.

### What does the app know about me?

Your account details and, while your purifier is connected, how much water it purifies, water quality readings, filter life and alert codes. You can switch this off in the app. The Privacy Policy has the full details.

## Returns and refunds

### I changed my mind. Can I send it back?

Yes, within 14 days of delivery, if it's unopened (full refund) or opened but never connected to water (minus a 10 percent restocking fee). You pay the return shipping when you change your mind. Once it's been installed, it can't be returned for a change of mind.

### How long until I get my money back?

After we receive and check the item, which takes up to 3 working days: 3 working days for mobile wallets, 5 for bank transfers, 7 for cash-on-delivery orders (paid by bank transfer) and 7 to 10 for cards.

## Service and maintenance

### Do you have a maintenance plan?

Yes, for AquaPearl purifiers. Basic (PKR 6,500 a year) includes one service visit, Plus (PKR 11,900) includes two visits and two pre-filters, and Premium (PKR 18,500) includes three visits, all scheduled filter changes and all parts. The Service & Maintenance Agreement has the details.

### How do I get a technician to look at my purifier?

Book through the Pearlz Home app, WhatsApp or the website. Visits for warranty problems are free. Outside the warranty, a visit costs PKR 2,500 in metro and secondary cities, but we waive it if you go ahead with the repair during the visit.

## AeroPearl Mini air purifier

### How big a room can the AeroPearl Mini clean?

It is designed for rooms up to 25 square metres.

### Can I wash the HEPA filter?

No, the HEPA filter can't be washed; replace it every 12 months. You can wash the mesh pre-screen in front of it, and you should do that once a month.
