# Pearlz Warranty Policy

Version 3.1. Last updated: 1 April 2026.

This policy applies to Pearlz products bought in Pakistan from the Pearlz online store or from an authorised Pearlz retail partner.

## 1. What the warranty promises

If a Pearlz product develops a defect in materials or workmanship during its warranty period, Pearlz will repair it or, at its choice, replace it, free of charge. Warranty repairs include parts, labour and the technician's visit.

## 2. Warranty periods

| Product | Warranty period | What the period applies to |
|---|---|---|
| AquaPearl 500 Pro | 24 months | Purifier body, pump and electronics |
| AquaPearl 300 | 12 months | Purifier body, pump and electronics |
| AeroPearl Mini | 12 months; fan motor 24 months | Body and electronics; the fan motor has the longer period |
| AquaPearl 250 (discontinued) | 36 months, Extended Care | See Section 6 |
| AeroPearl One (discontinued) | 36 months, Extended Care | See Section 6 |

## 3. What is covered and what is not

### 3.1 What is covered

The warranty covers defects in:

- the purifier body and housing;
- the pump;
- the electronics: control board, sensors, display, Wi-Fi module and, on the AquaPearl 500 Pro, the UV-LED module;
- on AeroPearl air purifiers, the fan motor.

### 3.2 Exclusions

The warranty does not cover:

1. **Consumables.** Filter cartridges of every kind (including PF-1, CB-2, RO-3, MN-4, CP-1, RO-2, PC-3 and the AeroPearl HEPA and carbon filters) and sanitising tablets are consumables with a limited service life. They are not covered.
2. **Physical damage.** Damage caused by dropping, impact or crushing, including a cracked or dented housing, after the product has been delivered to you.
3. **Unauthorised repair.** Any product that has been opened, repaired or modified by anyone other than a Pearlz-authorised technician. This exclusion applies to the whole product, not only to the part that was opened.
4. **Commercial use.** Products used anywhere other than a single private household, for example in offices, shops, schools or restaurants.
5. **Water-quality damage.** Damage caused by feed water outside the operating range in the Installation Guide: TDS above 2,000 ppm, temperature above 38 °C, or pressure above 4.0 bar without a Pearlz pressure-reducing valve.
6. **Incorrect installation.** Damage caused by installation that does not follow the AquaPearl Installation Guide. Section 7 of the Installation Guide lists the self-installation mistakes that void the warranty.
7. **Non-genuine parts.** Damage caused by cartridges, fittings or spare parts not supplied by Pearlz.
8. **Other property and indirect loss.** The warranty covers the Pearlz product only. It does not cover loss of or damage to other property, such as furniture, cabinets, walls or flooring, or any indirect or consequential loss.
9. **Cosmetic wear.** Scratches, fading and normal wear that do not affect how the product works.

## 4. How the warranty period is calculated

### 4.1 Start date

The warranty period starts on the date shown on your invoice. It does not start on the delivery date or the installation date.

### 4.2 If you do not have the invoice

If the invoice cannot be produced, the warranty period is calculated from the manufacturing date printed on the product's rating label.

### 4.3 Repairs and replacements

A repair or replacement does not restart or extend the original warranty period. Parts fitted under the warranty are covered for the rest of the original period or for 3 months, whichever is longer.

## 5. If you sell or give away your product

The warranty transfers to the new owner for the remainder of the original period, provided the new owner can show the original invoice. The period is not reset. To register the change of owner, email support@pearlzhome.example with the invoice and the serial number.

## 6. Discontinued models: Extended Care warranty

The AquaPearl 250 (discontinued on 30 June 2024) and the AeroPearl One (discontinued on 31 December 2023) were sold with a 36-month Extended Care warranty covering the body, pump or fan motor, and electronics. Pearlz honours these warranties in full until they end.

Pearlz keeps spare parts for the AquaPearl 250 until 30 June 2029 and for the AeroPearl One until 31 December 2028. If a covered repair is not possible because a part is no longer available, Pearlz replaces the product free of charge with its current equivalent: an AquaPearl 300 for an AquaPearl 250, and an AeroPearl Mini for an AeroPearl One.

The exclusions in Section 3.2 apply to Extended Care warranties in the same way.

## 7. How to make a warranty claim

Have these ready before you contact us:

- your invoice, or your order number if you bought from the Pearlz online store;
- the product's serial number, from the rating label on the back panel or from the Pearlz Home app under Settings, then Device info;
- a description of the fault and, where possible, a photo or short video.

Submit your claim by email to support@pearlzhome.example, by WhatsApp on +92 300 0732759, or through the contact form on the Pearlz website.

The claim procedure, including the inspection visit, the time limits Pearlz works to and how the repair or replacement decision is made, is set out in Section 6 of the Pearlz Service & Maintenance Agreement.

## 8. After the warranty ends

Repairs after the warranty period, or repairs of damage excluded by Section 3.2, are charged at the rates in Section 7 of the Service & Maintenance Agreement. A maintenance plan under that agreement can also cover parts and labour after the warranty ends.

## 9. Your other rights

This warranty is in addition to any rights you have under the law.
