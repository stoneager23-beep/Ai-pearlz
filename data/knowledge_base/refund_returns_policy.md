# Pearlz Refund & Returns Policy

Version 2.3. Last updated: 15 February 2026.

This policy applies to products bought from the Pearlz online store, by phone or by WhatsApp. Products bought from a retail partner are returned to that retailer under its own policy; the Pearlz warranty still applies to them.

## 1. Return window

You can return a product within **14 days of the delivery date** shown on the courier's delivery confirmation. Requests received after 14 days are handled under the Warranty Policy, not as returns.

## 2. Condition of the product

| Condition | Can it be returned? | Refund |
|---|---|---|
| Unopened, in its original sealed packaging | Yes | Full refund of the price paid |
| Opened but unused: never connected to water, with all accessories and the original packaging | Yes | Price paid minus a 10 percent restocking fee |
| Installed, connected to water, or used | Not for a change of mind | Not refundable; if it is faulty, the Warranty Policy applies |
| Damaged on arrival, faulty on arrival, or not what you ordered | Yes | Free replacement, or a full refund if you prefer |

Damage on arrival must be reported within 48 hours of delivery, with photos of the product and the packaging.

## 3. Who pays return shipping

- **Faulty, damaged or wrong item:** Pearlz arranges and pays for the return pickup.
- **Change of mind:** you pay for return shipping. If you use a Pearlz pickup, PKR 500 (metro cities) or PKR 800 (secondary cities) is deducted from your refund.
- Return pickups are available only in the metro and secondary-city tiers defined in the Pearlz Shipping Policy. In remote areas, send the return to the Pearlz warehouse by courier yourself, using the address on your return authorisation. Pearlz refunds that cost only when the item was faulty, damaged or wrong.

## 4. How long refunds take

Pearlz inspects every returned item within 3 working days of receiving it. Once the return is approved, refunds are issued as follows:

| How you paid | Refund method | Time after approval |
|---|---|---|
| Debit or credit card | Back to the same card | 7 to 10 working days |
| Bank transfer | Back to the same account | 5 working days |
| Cash on delivery | Bank transfer to an account you nominate | 7 working days |
| Mobile wallet | Back to the same wallet | 3 working days |
| Pearlz gift voucher | New voucher (store credit) | 1 working day |

Original delivery charges are refunded only when the item was faulty, damaged or wrong.

## 5. What cannot be refunded

- Filter cartridges and sanitising tablets once their seal or packaging has been opened.
- Professional installation, once the installation visit has taken place.
- Maintenance plans, which follow the cancellation terms in the Service & Maintenance Agreement.
- Items sold as clearance and marked "final sale".
- Pearlz gift vouchers.

## 6. Partial refunds and restocking fees

- A 10 percent restocking fee applies to products returned opened but unused for a change of mind.
- Missing accessories, manuals or packaging are deducted at their list price in the Pearlz Pricing Guide.
- If an item comes back damaged beyond what normal inspection would cause, Pearlz contacts you with the proposed deduction before any refund is issued.

## 7. Return, replacement or warranty repair: which applies?

- **Return:** you send the product back within 14 days and get your money back. For a change of mind, the product must be unused (Section 2).
- **Replacement:** a damaged item reported within 48 hours of delivery, or a faulty or wrong item reported within 14 days, is exchanged for a new one at no cost.
- **Warranty repair:** after 14 days, or for a product that has been installed, a fault is repaired or the product replaced under the Warranty Policy. Warranty repairs do not result in a refund.

## 8. Promotional bundles

- To get a refund for a bundle, return the whole bundle. You are refunded the price you paid for it.
- If you return only part of a bundle, the bundle discount no longer applies. Your refund is the price you paid for the bundle minus the list price of the items you keep.
- A free gift that came with a bundle must be returned with it, or its list price is deducted from your refund.

## 9. How to start a return

Open your order in the Pearlz Home app or on the website and choose Return an item, or contact support. You will receive a return authorisation number. Pack the product securely, preferably in its original packaging, and write the return authorisation number on the outside of the box.
