# AquaPearl Installation Guide

For the AquaPearl 500 Pro and the AquaPearl 300

**Revision B (January 2026).**

Pearlz Home Systems (Pvt.) Ltd. Customer support: support@pearlzhome.example  |  Toll-free 0800-73275  |  WhatsApp +92 300 0732759

> Read the whole guide before you start. If you are not comfortable working with water fittings and a drill, book a professional installation (Section 8).

## 1. Before you begin

You can install an AquaPearl purifier yourself or have a Pearlz technician install it. Installing it yourself does not affect your warranty, as long as you follow this guide. Section 7 lists the mistakes during self-installation that do void the warranty.

A typical self-installation takes 60 to 90 minutes. Both models can be mounted on a wall next to the sink. The AquaPearl 300 can also be placed inside the cabinet under the sink. The AquaPearl 500 Pro cannot be installed under the sink, because its display, control panel and nozzle must stay within reach; it can be wall-mounted or stood on a stable countertop.

The AquaPearl 500 Pro and the AquaPearl 300 install the same way, except where this guide says otherwise. The AquaPearl 500 Pro has a FLUSH button and an 8-litre tank; the AquaPearl 300 has no FLUSH button and a 6-litre tank.

## 2. Pre-installation checklist

Check every item before you start. If any item cannot be met, do not install the purifier; contact Pearlz support for advice.

| Requirement | What you need |
|---|---|
| Water supply | Cold municipal drinking-water line with a 1/2-inch connection near the sink |
| Inlet water pressure | 1.0 to 4.0 bar. Above 4.0 bar, fit a Pearlz pressure-reducing valve. Below 1.0 bar, a booster pump is needed, which is fitted only by a Pearlz technician. |
| Inlet water temperature | 5 to 38 °C. Never connect to a hot-water line. |
| Inlet water TDS | 2,000 ppm or less |
| Power socket | Grounded three-pin socket, 220-240 V, 50 Hz, within 1.2 metres of the purifier. No extension leads. |
| Drain | A sink drain pipe with at least 30 cm of straight vertical pipe for the drain saddle clamp |
| Space for wall mounting | A solid wall that can hold 25 kg, with 15 cm free above the purifier and 5 cm on each side for filter access |
| Space under the sink (AquaPearl 300 only) | Cabinet interior of at least 45 cm high, 30 cm wide and 45 cm deep |

### 2.1 How to check your water pressure

Ask your building manager, or measure it with a pressure gauge on an outside tap. As a rough check, if a 1-litre bottle under your kitchen tap fully open takes longer than 10 seconds to fill, your pressure may be below 1.0 bar.

### 2.2 How to check your water's TDS

Use a digital TDS meter pen in a glass of tap water. Readings above 2,000 ppm mean your water is outside the purifier's operating range.

## 3. Tools and parts

You will need:

- Electric drill with a 6 mm masonry bit (wall mounting only) and a 6 mm metal bit (for the drain saddle)
- 12 mm drill bit suitable for your countertop (AquaPearl 300 under-sink installation only)
- Phillips screwdriver
- Adjustable spanner
- PTFE thread-seal tape
- Tube cutter or a sharp utility knife
- Spirit level
- Towel and a bucket

All fittings, tubes and the wall bracket are supplied in the box. Use only the supplied fittings and tubes.

## 4. Wall-mount installation

1. Close the main cold-water valve under the sink and open the kitchen tap to release the pressure.
2. Hold the wall bracket where the purifier will hang, with its top edge at least 15 cm below any cabinet. Check it is level, mark the four holes and drill them with the 6 mm masonry bit.
3. Insert the four wall plugs and screw the bracket to the wall.
4. Fit the feed-water valve: unscrew the cold-water hose from the tap's supply pipe, wrap the threads of the feed-water valve with three turns of PTFE tape, screw the valve onto the pipe and reconnect the hose to the valve. Keep the valve's small lever closed.
5. Fit the drain saddle clamp to the vertical drain pipe at least 15 cm above the trap, and drill a 6 mm hole through the pipe wall through the clamp's opening.
6. Cut the white inlet tube to length with a straight, clean cut. Push one end firmly into the feed-water valve and the other into the purifier's inlet port until it stops, about 15 mm.
7. Push the red drain tube into the drain port and into the drain saddle clamp in the same way.
8. Hang the purifier on the bracket and check that it sits level.
9. Open the main cold-water valve and the feed-water valve. Check every connection for drips.
10. Plug in the purifier and carry out the first-run flushing procedure in Section 6.

## 5. Under-sink installation

Under-sink installation is possible for the AquaPearl 300 only.

1. Empty the cabinet and check that it meets the space requirements in Section 2.
2. Fit the feed-water valve and the drain saddle clamp exactly as in steps 1, 4 and 5 of Section 4.
3. Place the purifier upright on the cabinet floor, at least 5 cm from the back wall so the tubes are not bent.
4. Connect the white inlet tube and the red drain tube as in steps 6 and 7 of Section 4. Do not let the tubes kink or rest against hot-water pipes.
5. Drill a 12 mm hole in the countertop or sink deck, fit the supplied countertop tap and connect it to the purifier's outlet with the blue tube.
6. Open the main cold-water valve and the feed-water valve. Check every connection for drips.
7. Plug in the purifier and carry out the first-run flushing procedure in Section 6.

> **NOTE:** Place a towel on the cabinet floor for the first 24 hours so that any drip is easy to see.

## 6. First-run flushing procedure

### 6.1 Why flushing is required

New cartridges are packed with tiny carbon particles from manufacturing, a food-grade preservative inside the RO membrane, and trapped air. Flushing washes these out to the drain before any water reaches your glass. Until a new cartridge has been flushed, the water can taste flat, plasticky or slightly bitter, smell faintly of carbon, and look cloudy or milky with fine bubbles. None of this means the purifier is faulty.

### 6.2 When to flush

Run the flushing procedure:

- after the purifier is first installed;
- after you replace any cartridge;
- after the purifier has not been used for more than 14 days;
- after it has been moved and reinstalled.

### 6.3 Flushing the AquaPearl 500 Pro

1. Make sure the feed-water valve is open and the purifier is switched on.
2. Hold FLUSH for 3 seconds. The display counts down from 10 minutes while water runs to the drain.
3. When the flush cycle ends, let the tank fill completely. This takes about 40 minutes.
4. Dispense and discard the whole first tank, 8 litres.
5. The water is now ready to drink.

### 6.4 Flushing the AquaPearl 300

The AquaPearl 300 has no FLUSH button.

1. Make sure the feed-water valve is open and the purifier is switched on.
2. Let the tank fill completely.
3. Dispense and discard the whole tank.
4. Repeat steps 2 and 3 once more, so that two full tanks (12 litres) are discarded.
5. The water is now ready to drink.

### 6.5 If the taste does not improve

If the water still tastes unusual after the flushing procedure, run it once more. If the taste persists after a second flush, contact Pearlz support.

## 7. What voids the warranty

Installing the purifier yourself is allowed. The warranty is void, however, for damage caused by any of the following during installation:

- connecting the purifier to a hot-water line, or to water warmer than 38 °C;
- running the purifier at an inlet pressure above 4.0 bar without a Pearlz pressure-reducing valve;
- drilling into, cutting or otherwise modifying the purifier housing, for example to fit your own brackets;
- using fittings, tubes or cartridges that are not supplied by Pearlz;
- dropping the purifier or cracking its housing while handling it;
- plugging the purifier into an ungrounded socket or through an extension lead.

If you are unsure whether your installation is correct, send photos to Pearlz support before switching the purifier on.

A Pearlz technician can install the purifier for you. The visit includes fitting the feed-water valve and drain connection, wall mounting or under-sink placement, a pressure and TDS check, the first-run flush, and a short demonstration.

## 9. After installation

- Check all connections for drips after 30 minutes and again after 24 hours.
- Keep the feed-water valve open during normal use.
- Register your purifier in the Pearlz Home app, or keep your invoice in a safe place; you will need it for any warranty claim.
- Expect the TDS reading to settle within the first two days of use.
